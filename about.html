About us

About our student-led organization

We are an independent Bay Area student volunteer group focused on tutoring, STEM, engineering, technology, and academic support.

Our story

Why we started

The group began with students who wanted to share skills they were already developing in math, science, engineering, computer science, writing, and college preparation.

Instead of deciding in advance what another community needs, we are reaching out directly to tribes, tribal education departments, and Native organizations. We ask where additional student support could be useful, discuss what our volunteers can realistically provide, and work toward a manageable first project.

When an organization asks for orientation, cultural competency learning, background requirements, or other onboarding, we will follow its process.

How we work

Listen before planning

Community partners know their students, priorities, and context. We begin with their perspective.

Be honest about capacity

We only offer projects our volunteers have the skills and time to support responsibly.

Begin with something manageable

A focused workshop or short tutoring pilot gives everyone room to learn what works.
